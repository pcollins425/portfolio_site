Use case

A project is the job that changes a floor.

A project is its own record. An install, a removal, a move, or a conversion stays on the project: when it runs, which casino it is at, and which units, cabinets, and themes are on the job.

It is not the casino. The casino keeps the property, the performance, and the floor. The project points at that casino so you can see what is scheduled there and what changed the floor.

It is not the asset. Each unit on the job is already a record. The project points at those assets, and at the cabinets and themes on them. From an asset you can see which project put it there, moved it, converted it, or took it off.

An agreement points at the same assets. The project is how those units were placed or pulled. The agreement is which deal covers them. You do not retype the serial list onto either record.

A work order is a different kind of work. The project changes what is on the floor. The work order is service on a unit. They can meet on the same asset, and they stay separate records.

Your database stays on your servers. Your data.
