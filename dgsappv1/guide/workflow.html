Workflow

Run the job that changes the floor.

An install, a removal, a move, or a conversion is a job. You see when it runs, which property it is at, and which machines, cabinets, and themes are on it.

From the casino you see what is scheduled there and what already changed the floor. From a machine you see which job placed it, moved it, or took it off. The agreement tells you which deal those machines sit on, so the job and the customer stay in step.

This is not service. Fixing a machine that is already on the floor is maintenance. The job changes what the floor is. After it lands, performance is how those machines do.

Your jobs stay on your servers. Your data.
