Use case

An agreement points at the assets it covers.

An agreement is its own record. The units it covers are assets, not a serial number typed onto the deal a second time. From each asset you can see the cabinet it is, the casino it sits at, and the parts that cabinet takes.

A company that places games and a company that also sells parts can use the same link. The agreement does not have to become the parts list, and the parts list does not have to become the deal.

A project is often how those units get onto a floor or come off it. The project points at the assets. The agreement points at the assets. You meet on the unit: which job placed or pulled it, and which agreement covers it.

A work order is service on one of those units. It points at the asset. It does not get copied onto the agreement as a second history.

Your database stays on your servers. Your data.
