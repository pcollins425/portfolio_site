Use case

The cabinet and the asset are different records.

A cabinet and its specs are one entity. The asset is a particular machine, even when it is that cabinet. The specs stay the specs when the unit moves. The asset is what sits on a floor, in a warehouse, or on a job.

You arrive here from a casino, by picking a machine on that floor. From the asset you can see how that cabinet has performed, which theme is on it, and where else that theme is installed. You can see the agreement the unit sits on, and the parts that cabinet takes.

A project points at this asset when the job installed it, moved it, converted it, or removed it. The project keeps the job: when, which casino, and what action. The asset keeps the fact that it was on that job. Those stay two records.

A work order points at this asset. It is service on this unit. Through the asset you reach the cabinet, the casino the unit is at, and the parts that cabinet takes. The work order is not the project that placed or pulled the bank.

Your database stays on your servers. Your data.
