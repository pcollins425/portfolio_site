Performance

A result you can explain.

A floor’s numbers are only useful when they still describe that floor. If installs, pulls, and down time live somewhere else, a weak month might be a bad game, a bank that landed halfway through the month, or a cabinet that was out of service. The report itself cannot say which.

Performance is connected to the machines that were actually there, the theme on them, and the job that put them there or took them off. A theme that looks strong can be followed to the other floors where it sits, on which cabinets, and in which regions. A soft cabinet can be read next to the service on that unit, so a poor game and a machine that was down are not the same conclusion.

That is the benefit for the people who have to answer for the month. The number and the reason are one story. The customer conversation can use that same story, because the agreement covers those same machines.

The results stay on your servers. Why that matters.
