Data

Your data stays on your server.

This site is collinsmediallc.com. That address is the door. The pages are hosted here so approved people can open the application.

Performance, maintenance, customers, and jobs read the same database. It sits on your own servers. Opening this site does not place a copy of that database on the website.

People sign in with your company’s email, and that sign-in opens your server only. Your server is the one that accepts your people. Another company using this same door is sent to its own server. Its login can open only that server.

Because it is one database, a move does not fork the information. The cabinet’s specs stay the cabinet’s specs. You update where the unit is, which job moved it, and which agreement covers it. The next person who asks how it performed, what parts it takes, or which customer it sits with is looking at that same floor.

The system of record stays with you. The shared site is the directory that points a company at its own server.
