CMMS · maintenance

Service the machine in front of you.

You find the unit. You see the cabinet it is, so the parts in front of you are the parts that cabinet takes, not a catalog you have to match by eye.

Service stays on that unit. A work order is the record of work done to that machine, at the property where it sits. You can come at it from the floor — what was serviced at this casino — or from the machine — what has been done to this one.

Maintenance is not the job that changed the floor. An install, a removal, a move, or a conversion is workflow. A work order is service on a machine that is already there. They can involve the same unit. They are different kinds of work.

If you need to know whether that machine is earning, that question is performance. If you need the agreement that covers it, that is the customer.

Your service history stays on your servers. Your data.
