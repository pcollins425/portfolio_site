CMMS · maintenance

Service the machine the rest of the business already knows.

Maintenance gets expensive when the parts book and the machine on the floor have drifted apart. Someone matches a cabinet from memory. The parts that get used are a best guess, because the list was never tied to that unit.

Connected to the floor, the machine being serviced is the cabinet on that floor, so the parts are the parts that cabinet takes. That same unit is the one performance is watching, so service sits next to how the machine has been running. The benefit is the parts on the right cabinet, and a clear read of that machine while the work is happening.

A work order is that service, kept on the unit. An install, a removal, or a move is a different kind of work. That job stays with workflow, so a floor change is not written down as a repair, and a repair is not mistaken for a new install.
