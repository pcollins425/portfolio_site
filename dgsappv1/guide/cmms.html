CMMS · maintenance

Service the machine the rest of the business already knows.

Maintenance gets expensive when the parts book, the machine on the floor, and the work history are three lists that drifted. Someone matches a cabinet from memory. Later, nobody can tell whether a machine that earned poorly was down, or whether the game was the problem.

Connected to the floor, the unit being serviced is the same unit performance is scoring and the same unit the agreement covers. The cabinet is known, so the parts are the parts that cabinet takes. The property is known, so the work is attached to the customer it was done for. Service sitting next to results is how a one-off repair becomes different from a cabinet that keeps failing.

A work order is that service, kept on the unit. An install, a removal, or a move is a different kind of work. That job stays with workflow, so a floor change is not written down as a repair, and a repair is not mistaken for a new install.
