Use case

Start with a casino.

A casino is its own record. Performance, the floor, and the history of what has been there are reached from that record, because that is where the question starts.

You look up the property. From there you can see how it has performed, open the floor as it stands, and look at what has been on it. Then you pick a machine and continue to that asset.

A project at that casino is the job that changes the floor: an install, a removal, a move, a conversion. The project points at this casino and at the assets on the job. You can see what is scheduled and what changed the floor. The project stays its own record. It is not a second copy of the casino.

A work order here is service on a unit at this property. It points at the asset. The casino is one place that service shows up, because the asset is on this floor. The work order is not the project that placed or pulled the bank.

From the machine you picked, the next page is the asset: the cabinet it is, the theme on it, the agreement it sits on, and the parts that cabinet takes.

Your database stays on your servers. Your data.
