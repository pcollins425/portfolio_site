Work the customer from the property.

You look up the casino. That is the customer in front of you: what agreement covers the machines, which vendor is behind the cabinets, and what is actually on the floor.

A company that builds games, a company that places them, and a company that sells parts can start in the same place. The commercial relationship and the floor are one customer. You do not keep a deal in one system and the machines in another.

From the agreement you see the units it covers. From a unit you see the cabinet, the property, and the parts that cabinet takes. If those units were just placed or pulled, the job is how the floor changed. How the house is earning is performance. Service on a unit is maintenance.

Your customers stay on your servers. Your data.
