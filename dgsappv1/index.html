Separate lists, linked on purpose.

Machines, agreements, locations, cabinets, parts, and results each stay in their own list. A record in one list points at a record in another. That pointer is a reference: the agreement refers to a machine that exists, and the parts refer to the cabinet that machine is. This is a relational database — separate lists that stay related, because each link points at a real record.

What that gives you

  • Follow one path. Where the unit is, which agreement covers it, which parts it takes, and how it performed.
  • Change it once. A move, a conversion, or a cabinet change is made on the record the other lists are pointing at.
  • Ask a new question without a new copy. The links are already there. A new report follows them.
