The help is in the connection.

A performance number is hard to trust when the installs, the pulls, and the service live in other files. Maintenance is guesswork when the parts book and the machine on the floor have drifted apart. A customer meeting goes sideways when the agreement describes a different floor than the one in the building.

Connecting them means performance, maintenance, and the customer are reading the same machines. The job that placed or pulled those machines stays in that same database, so a change in the month sits next to the result. And that database stays on your servers, which is what makes it reasonable to keep all of it together.

Your data stays on your server.

Which casinos you serve, which themes earn, and how your floors are serviced is yours. Holding it locally keeps that from becoming someone else’s asset. This site is collinsmediallc.com. That address is the door.

Your performance, your customers, your jobs, and your service history sit in a database on your own servers. People sign in with your company's email, and that sign-in opens your server only. Another company using this same door is sent to its own server. Its login can open only that server.
