Problem

A chat window next to the business is not a pipeline. Without routing, context, and write-back rules, AI stays a side demo.

What you get

Design multi-step flows: retrieve the right records, call a model, clarify when unsure, apply or draft only where allowed, and log what happened. Tie that to your APIs and permissions.

Where it shows up

Assistant-style flows in production ops tooling use the same idea — model help on top of authenticated data, not a free-floating bot.
